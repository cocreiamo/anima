# Licensing and brand

The rules for the licence of Anima, of what it adopts and of what is built on
it, and for its names. The legal texts are the source (`LICENSE`, `CLA.md`,
`PLUGIN-EXCEPTION.md`, `TRADEMARKS.md`); this skill holds the rules and the
reasons. The legal name of the copyright holder, Cocreiamo di Bilotta
Francesco (a sole proprietorship, to be succeeded by a company), may appear
here and in the legal texts; no other person is named.

## Use this when

- Adding or replacing a component, and reading its licence.
- Accepting or asking for a contribution.
- Writing a plugin, or deciding whether something is core or plugin.
- Naming a product, a domain, a package or an offer; writing about hosting Anima.

## Rules

1. **Anima is AGPL-3.0-only, and Cocreiamo also offers commercial terms.** Anima is licensed under the GNU Affero General Public License, version 3 only (`LICENSE`); Cocreiamo offers commercial licences to those who cannot meet its terms (dual licensing), and commits publicly that the core stays available under the AGPL. Not: a source-available licence (FSL, BSL, Elastic License, SSPL, "internal use only" terms): it contradicts the claim of being fully open source and the agency model.
2. **Contributions come only under the CLA (`CLA.md`).** It is the Apache Individual Contributor License Agreement 2.2, adapted: "The Foundation" becomes Cocreiamo, the signature form and the non-profit sentence are removed, and section 9 extends the agreement to successors and assigns, so that a future company takes over without contributors signing again. Not: a DCO sign-off alone; it grants no right to relicense, so dual licensing would be impossible. Code written by the founder personally passes to a company by a separate deed, not by the CLA.
3. **Sign the CLA on pull requests with CLA Assistant,** linked to the whole organization; the text it shows is a public Gist identical to `CLA.md` and must change with it (every contributor then signs again). The allowlist is the founder, who is the copyright holder, and bots, which cannot sign and whose pull requests the founder reviews and merges. Not: the contributor-assistant GitHub Action; it is archived.
4. **A forked component keeps the licence of the original software, and so does every change to it.** Changes to the database server's fork are under the PostgreSQL License, so they can be contributed upstream as they are (`anima-maintaining-a-fork`). "Every modification of the core is AGPL" applies to Anima's own code, not to forked components.
5. **A shipped component declares an allowed SPDX licence.** The allowlist is `policy.allowed_licenses` of `components.toml`, checked by a test (R17); adding an identifier is the founder's decision; an excluded licence ends the discussion (HashiCorp Vault, under the Business Source License, was excluded for that). Example: a distribution image whose packages carry their own licences is declared with a `LicenseRef`, never with one SPDX identifier that would claim more than it says; the founder confirmed on 2026-11-27 that `LicenseRef-Fedora-Distribution` is allowed for the base image of the engine.
6. **Every file carries copyright and licence information (REUSE, R18).** `REUSE.toml` has a catch-all annotation and an `override` for each adopted text, naming the upstream authors and licence; an adopted text is adopted verbatim or says what was changed (`anima-repository-conventions`).
7. **Configuration and data are not covered by the licence.** The boundary is the one between the engine, which the product owns, and an instance's configuration, which the adopter owns (`anima-configuration-design`).
8. **A plugin may carry any licence.** The Anima Plugin Exception (`PLUGIN-EXCEPTION.md`) is an additional permission under section 7 of the AGPL: a plugin, a work that uses Anima only through its public interfaces, may carry any licence, and so may code generated by Anima's own tools. Every modification of the core is AGPL, including one made so that a plugin can work. Official plugins live in the core repository and are AGPL. The architecture of the boundary is ADR 0002.
9. **"Anima" is the runtime and "Cocreiamo" is the house brand.** The marks COCREIAMO and ANIMA are owned by Cocreiamo di Bilotta Francesco and rest on use until registered; registration at the EU office in classes 9 and 42 is planned. The policy is `TRADEMARKS.md`, derived from the Model Trademark Guidelines (CC BY 4.0, attributed in the file); the contact is in that file. Not: coined alternative names; they were rejected and the original record states no reason.
10. **Say what the policy allows.** Free: factual statements such as "plugin for Anima", "compatible with Cocreiamo", "runs on Anima". Licence required: the marks in the name or branding of a hosting or SaaS offer, and the words official, certified, partner. Not allowed: the marks in domain names and product names. The AGPL lets anyone host Anima, so hosting under one's own brand needs no permission and presenting the service as Anima or Cocreiamo does; a service run by the owner needs no licence, and a written agreement is needed if it becomes a separate company or is sold. The trademark, not a name prefix, is the commercial lever and protects the brand, which is why names in the repository are unbranded.
11. Unverified: **the signature of the CLA on pull requests and the privacy notice for it.** CLA Assistant is a GitHub App setting and records the user name and signing date of each signer, so a privacy notice must be linked before the first outside contribution; neither is verified by a test of this repository (R17 and R18 are).
12. Unverified: **the registration of the marks.** It is planned and has not been done.

## Questions to answer before proposing

- Which SPDX identifier does it declare, and is it on the allowlist?
- Does a text of ours adopt another's text, and does `REUSE.toml` name its authors and licence?
- Is the thing core or plugin, and does it use only public interfaces?
- Does a name or a domain use a mark in a way the policy does not allow?

## Where the result goes

The rules stay here; the legal texts are the source. The allowlist and licences go to `components.toml`, the annotations to `REUSE.toml`, the licence texts to `LICENSES/`. The architectural boundary of plugins is ADR 0002. Related skills: `anima-adopting-a-component`, `anima-repository-conventions`.
