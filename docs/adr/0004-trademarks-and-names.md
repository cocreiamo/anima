# Trademarks and names

## Context and Problem Statement

The AGPL lets anyone host Anima ([ADR-0002](0002-licensing-and-cla.md)). What the licence does not give
away is the name. The trademark is therefore the commercial lever for hosted
services and for the words that signal endorsement.

Which names and which trademark policy let the trademark serve as the commercial lever for hosted services, given that the AGPL lets anyone host Anima?

## Decision Drivers

- The AGPL lets anyone host Anima ([ADR-0002](0002-licensing-and-cla.md)); what the licence does not give
  away is the name.
- The trademark is the commercial lever for hosted services and for the
  words that signal endorsement.

## Considered Options

- "Anima" for the runtime and "Cocreiamo" for the house brand
- Coined alternative names

## Decision Outcome

Chosen option: "Anima" for the runtime and "Cocreiamo" for the house brand, with the policy of `TRADEMARKS.md`. The original record gives no other justification.

1. Names: "Anima" is the runtime, "Cocreiamo" is the house brand.
2. Marks: COCREIAMO™ and ANIMA™, owned by Cocreiamo di Bilotta Francesco, not
   yet registered. Registration at EUIPO is planned in classes 9 and 42.
3. Policy: [TRADEMARKS.md](../../TRADEMARKS.md), derived from the Model
   Trademark Guidelines 1.0 (CC BY 4.0, attributed in the file).
   - Free use: factual statements such as "plugin for Anima", "compatible
     with Cocreiamo", "runs on Anima".
   - Licence required: the marks in the name or branding of a hosting or SaaS
     offer, and the words official, certified, partner.
   - Not allowed: the marks in domain names and product names.
4. Contact: trademarks@cocreiamo.com.

### Consequences

- Neutral, because hosting Anima under one's own brand needs no permission;
  presenting the service as Anima or Cocreiamo does.
- Neutral, because a service run by the owner needs no licence. A written
  agreement is needed if that service becomes a separate company or is sold.
- Bad, because until registration, the marks rest on use only.
