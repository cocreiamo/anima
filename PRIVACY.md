# Privacy notice for the contributor agreement

This notice says what personal data is processed when you sign the
[Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md) (the CLA) of Cocreiamo, who handles
it, and what your rights are. It covers only the signing of the CLA. For
anything else on GitHub, see the
[GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Who is responsible

Cocreiamo, as named in the [CLA](CLA.md), decides why and how the signatures
are recorded and is the controller for them. Cocreiamo is established in
Italy, in the European Union.

## What is collected, and why

You sign the CLA on your first pull request, through CLA Assistant, an
open-source tool that runs as a GitHub App. It records:

- your GitHub user name and numeric GitHub user identifier;
- the repository (and its owner) you signed for, the version of the CLA text
  you signed, and the date and time you signed;
- the address of the CLA text and how you signed (the tool's `origin` field).

For you as the signed-in user, the tool's operator also holds the numbers of
the pull requests it checked for you and the sign-in token that GitHub gives
the tool when you sign in with GitHub, which it uses to read your pull
requests and to post its comment.

CLA Assistant can also ask for more fields (for example a name or an e-mail
address) when its owner configures a form. Cocreiamo does not configure one,
so it asks you for nothing beyond your GitHub sign-in.

The purpose is to keep proof that you agreed to the CLA, so that Cocreiamo
can show it holds the right to distribute your contribution, including under
commercial terms. The data is not used for anything else, not for marketing
and not for profiling.

## Legal basis

Processing is necessary for the legitimate interests of Cocreiamo and its
users in having proof of the licence under which each contribution was made
(Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR). The data is minimal, it is already public on
your GitHub profile, and you sign knowingly. You may object: see below.

## Where it is kept, and who processes it

CLA Assistant is provided as a hosted service, `cla-assistant.io`, by SAP SE.
Its documentation states that the data is stored in a database hosted on
Microsoft Azure in Europe. SAP SE, which runs the service, and GitHub, which
signs you in, process your data under their own privacy statements:

- [Privacy statement of cla-assistant.io](https://gist.github.com/CLAassistant/3a73e4cd729c9d0a6e30)
- [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)

According to its own privacy statement, SAP SE shares data with companies of
its group and with service providers, and where data leaves the European
Economic Area without an adequacy decision it relies on the standard
contractual clauses of the European Commission. Read its statement, linked
above, for the details; they are SAP SE's, not Cocreiamo's.

Cocreiamo does not keep a copy of the records in its repositories. Nobody
else receives them. A pull request, its comments and your commits are public
on GitHub whether or not you sign.

## Who can read it

The people who administer the organization's repositories on GitHub can see
the list of signers of each version of the CLA, and can export it. The
operator of the hosted service can access its database.

## How long it is kept

For as long as Cocreiamo may need to show that your contribution was made
under the CLA, which is as long as the contribution is distributed. The tool
sets no shorter period and Cocreiamo has no way to set one.

## Your rights

You may ask to see the data held about you, have it corrected, have it erased
or restricted, receive a copy, and object to the processing. Erasing a
signature removes the proof that your contribution was made under the CLA, so
Cocreiamo may have to remove or replace the contribution as well. You may
complain to a data protection authority; in Italy it is the Garante per la
protezione dei dati personali.

To use a right, open an issue in the
[issue tracker of this repository](https://github.com/cocreiamo/anima/issues)
titled "Privacy request". The issue is public, so it must contain no personal
data and no detail of your request. It only asks Cocreiamo for a private
channel; Cocreiamo answers there, and the request itself goes through that
channel. Cocreiamo answers within one month.
